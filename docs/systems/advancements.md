# Advancements

Sol's Relic System v1.5.8 includes a crafting advancement and a progression chain based on collecting its rare materials.

## Aster Architect

| Advancement | Frame | Trigger | Parent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aster Architect | Task | Craft an Aster Table | Vanilla Story: Iron Tools |

Crafting the table grants **Aster Architect**, shows its toast, and announces the achievement in chat. Merely obtaining or placing an Aster Table does not satisfy the requirement.

## Resonance progression

| Advancement | Frame | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| First Spark of Resonance | Task | Obtain Resonance Core I |
| Echoes Gather | Task | Obtain Resonance Core II |
| Harmonic Current | Goal | Obtain Resonance Core III |
| Gilded Resonance | Goal | Obtain Resonance Core IV |
| Mythic Convergence | Challenge | Obtain Resonance Core V |
| Supreme Resonance | Challenge | Obtain Resonance Core VI |
| Fate, Rewritten | Challenge | Obtain Dust of Enlightenment |
| Sixfold Sovereign | Challenge | Hold all six Resonance Core levels at once |

**First Spark of Resonance** is the visible root and supplies the advancement tree's proper background texture. The seven follow-up advancements stay hidden until their requirements are met. Every completed advancement shows a toast and announces in chat.

The chain tracks obtaining materials, not successfully performing the associated ascension or reroll. In v1.5.8, Forge 1.16.5 inventory triggers require the exact Resonance Core or Dust item; unrelated inventory changes no longer grant the progression chain.
